Coverage Tracker v6.3

  1. Start AR, start the Insta360 recording, then tap Sync next to the camera.
  2. Optional: tape a mark on the floor, stand on it, tap Drift (set mark).
  3. Optional: tap Area, walk the outer edge, tap Finish.
  4. Walk your passes. Set Stick height to Low / Mid / High for each pass.
  5. Back on the tape mark: tap Drift to measure, then Fix to correct the map.
  6. Outdoors, GPS records the location during the first minute and then switches itself off (for the map viewer). Indoors it is skipped.
  7. ☰ has the settings and Exit. Export appears here after you exit.

Settings

Map coloursPasses: how often you walked each cell. Heights: how many stick heights covered it.
Map rotationFollow me: the map turns with you, up is where you're facing. Fixed: up stays the start direction.
Floor tilesColoured tiles drawn on the real floor in the camera view.
Sync signalChirp: a sound the Insta360 records. Flash: silent, the screen flashes white, hold it in front of the lens.
Speed limit0.5 indoors, low light or close objects. 1.0 outdoors in daylight. The bar turns red above it.
Map placement (GPS)Records location at the start for the map viewer, then switches off.
Eco modeSaves battery: updates the map and speed bar less often, and lowers AR resolution from the next start. Floor tiles stay.
ResetClears this session's map, trail, checkpoint and area. Tap twice.
Exit AREnds the session. The export button appears on the start screen.
